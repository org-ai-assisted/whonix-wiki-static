Whonix Wiki Editing Policy

  • Copyright warning: Do not submit copyrighted work without permission!
  • Conditions apply: Read, understand, and agree to Conditions for Contributions to Whonix.
  • No ownership: If you do not want your writing to be edited mercilessly or redistributed freely, do not submit it here.
  • Non-acceptance notice: Submitting content does not guarantee acceptance or inclusion. Contributions may be rejected without explanation.
  • Future changes notice: You are contributing to a collaborative project. Content submitted here is not protected from changes.
  • Open editing policy: All contributions to this website may be edited, altered, or removed by other contributors at any time.
  • Original work required: You are also promising that you wrote this yourself. Before copying content from elsewhere, ask for approval from the admins in the Whonix forums.
  • Volunteer contributions: Want to improve this page? Help is welcome, and volunteer contributions are happily considered!
  • First-time contributors: Please read the Documentation Contributor Policy.
  • Moderation: Edits are held for moderation.
  • Policies: Policy of Website and Chat and Policy On Nonfreedom Software apply.
  • AI policy: Do not post AI-generated content in communications or source code contributions without clearly marking it as AI-generated. Policy On Artificial Intelligence (AI) applies.
